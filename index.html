Research software, benchmark and preprint

AI FPGA Engineer: Benchmarking LLM-Generated VHDL

The hdleval evaluation platform and a versioned VHDL benchmark suite. Released v1.0.

Can LLM-generated HDL be evaluated reliably without conflating compilation, synthesis, functional correctness and hardware feasibility?

216toolchain-backed evaluations
18benchmark circuits
11evaluation stages
54/54positive-control recovery
95%Wilson intervals on every rate
89%test line coverage

Key findings

  • The positive control scored 100% and three graded synthetic controls landed in their designed quality order (72.2%, 55.6%, 44.4%), with the overlap of adjacent intervals reported as the instrument's resolution limit.
  • All 69 failures were attributed to a single pipeline stage: 57 genuine compiler rejections and 12 absent outputs.
  • Grading the same designs with static structure checks alone passes 83 to 100% of them, against a true 44 to 72%. Static-only evaluation materially overstates HDL correctness.

Scope: the baseline validates the measurement instrument with a reference replay and seeded synthetic providers. It is not a ranking of commercial language models; real-model studies through the same harness are the next step.

Experiment baseline-v1 · 216 runs (4 providers × 18 circuits × 3 seeded trials) · GHDL and Yosys required on every run · benchmark suite v1 · reproduce with python reproduce.py or Docker
